### Short description (~100 words)

```
SpeechPrep helps you rehearse a wedding speech out loud and find out what it
actually sounds like, before the room hears it.

Most people prepare by reading the script silently a few times. That tells you
nothing about pace, and nothing about the lines you drop when you are nervous.
SpeechPrep listens while you run it, then gives you per-section timing, a diff
between what you wrote and what you said, and one specific thing to fix.

You can try it on a sample speech without an account. Your first full rehearsal
on your own speech is free.
```

### Full description (2 to 3 paragraphs)

```
SpeechPrep is a rehearsal tool for people who have been asked to speak at a
wedding. Best man, maid of honor, father of the bride: the writing is the part
everyone worries about, and the delivery is the part that actually goes wrong.

Paste or upload your speech and read it out loud. SpeechPrep times each section
against what you wrote, transcribes what you actually said, and shows you the
difference: the lines that ran long, the ones you skipped when your hands were
shaking, and the off-script moments that were better than the script. Then it
gives you one thing to work on before the next run, and follows the memorization
research on how to space those runs across the days before the wedding.

You can try the whole loop on a sample speech with no account. Your first full
rehearsal on your own speech is free, then a one-time Event Pass covers unlimited
rehearsals on that speech.
```

## Order of attack

From the PlanSeats field notes. This order is load-bearing, not arbitrary.

1. **SaaSHub first.** One free listing. Verify it for the green badge, attach ~10
   competitors so SpeechPrep appears on their alternatives pages. After editorial
   approval (free queue, up to ~32 days) it unlocks a free fan-out submit tool
   covering 100+ further directories. Everything downstream is cheaper if this
   goes first.
2. **AlternativeTo.** Can be live same day. Attach 10 alternatives.
3. **Indie Hackers.** Post as a product with the story.
4. **Launch calendar sites.** They schedule days to weeks out and give a lifetime
   dofollow link, so booking early costs nothing and the slot compounds.
5. **AI directories.** SpeechPrep qualifies on both counts (coaching and
   transcription).

## Findings from setting this up

- **There was no square logo.** Every directory needs one, minimum 512×512.
  Generated from `Logo.svg` and committed to `public/press/`.
- **The sitemap lists apex URLs, but the site serves www.** `speechprep.ai/x`
  301s to `www.speechprep.ai/x`, so every URL submitted from the sitemap
  redirects. Worth settling the canonical host before pointing dozens of
  directories at it, because those links are permanent. Note that `SITE_URL` is
  also what the auth redirect canonicalises to (ART-897), so changing it means
  updating the Supabase redirect allow list in the same change.
