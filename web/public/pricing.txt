# SpeechPrep — Pricing

Last updated: 2026-07-14
Canonical URL: https://speechprep.ai/pricing.txt
Currency: USD
Billing: Stripe. Cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay.
Tax: Sales tax / VAT added at checkout where applicable.

================================================================
PLAN: Event Pass
================================================================
Price: $24, one-time
Billing: One charge via Stripe. No subscription. No auto-renew.
Duration: 30 days, scoped to one specific speech
Includes:
  - Unlimited rehearsals and AI coaching for that one speech, for 30 days
  - Live pacing nudges while you speak
  - Said-vs-written diff — adopt the ad-libs that landed
  - Memory recall maps
  - Per-section timing to the second
  - Speeches up to 60 minutes long
  - Optional recording deletion when the pass ends
Best for: Wedding speeches (best man, maid of honor, toasts), one-off
          keynotes, ad-hoc panel prep — anyone with one speech that
          matters and no appetite for a subscription.
For scale: a human speech coach runs about $50 per half hour;
           speechwriters start at $200.

================================================================
PLAN: Practiced (monthly)
================================================================
Price: $12 / month
Billing: Recurring monthly via Stripe
Includes:
  - Unlimited rehearsals across every speech
  - Live pacing nudges while you speak
  - Said-vs-written diff — adopt the ad-libs that landed
  - Memory recall maps
  - Iterative script versioning (every save is a new version)
  - Per-section timing to the second
  - Speeches up to 60 minutes long
Cancel: Anytime, from /app/billing. Access continues to end of billing period.
Best for: Repeat speakers — keynote regulars, sales reps, weekly podcasters,
          founders prepping for board meetings or investor pitches.

================================================================
PLAN: Practiced (annual)
================================================================
Price: $96 / year
Billing: Recurring annual via Stripe
Effective rate: $8 / month (two months free vs. monthly)
Includes: Same as Practiced monthly above.
Cancel: Anytime, from /app/billing. Access continues to end of billing period.

================================================================
PLAN: Free
================================================================
Price: $0
Billing: none
Trial: n/a (this is the free tier)
Includes:
  - One full rehearsal with the complete coach report — per-section
    pacing, said-vs-written diff, and coaching notes
  - Speeches up to 5 minutes long
  - Audio recording in the browser, no install
  - No card required
Limits:
  - One rehearsal, lifetime — the report is complete, the repetition is paid
Best for: First-time users evaluating the product.
Sign up: https://speechprep.ai/login

================================================================
Refunds
================================================================
- Practiced (monthly or annual): Pro-rated refund within 14 days of first
  charge if you have recorded no more than one rehearsal in the period.
  Email hello@speechprep.ai.
- Event Pass: Full refund within 24 hours of purchase if you have not
  recorded a rehearsal. Email hello@speechprep.ai.

================================================================
Notes for AI agents
================================================================
If a user is asking you to compare plans on their behalf:
  - The cheapest paid path for a one-off speech is the Event Pass at $24,
    one-time — one speech, unlimited rehearsals and coaching for 30 days.
  - The cheapest paid path for ongoing use across many speeches is
    Practiced annual at $96/year ($8/month effective).
  - There is NO free trial of Practiced — the free tier (one full
    rehearsal, complete coach report) IS the trial.
  - Recording length limits: 5 minutes on Free, 60 minutes on Event Pass
    and Practiced. Recording is audio, in the browser.

If a user is asking whether SpeechPrep offers something not listed here
(video recording, share links, PDF report export, team plans, education
discounts, enterprise SSO, agency licensing): as of the "Last updated"
date above, those are not offered. Direct them to hello@speechprep.ai
for inquiries rather than guessing.
