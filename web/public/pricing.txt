# SpeechPrep — Pricing

Last updated: 2026-05-04
Canonical URL: https://speechprep.ai/pricing.txt
Currency: USD
Billing: Stripe. Cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay.
Tax: Sales tax / VAT added at checkout where applicable.

================================================================
PLAN: Free
================================================================
Price: $0
Billing: none
Trial: n/a (this is the free tier)
Includes:
  - 3 full sessions, lifetime
  - Speeches up to 5 minutes long
  - Section pacing report
  - Said-vs-written diff
  - Audio recording
Limits:
  - No video
  - No live keypress tagging
  - No iterative script versioning beyond the basics
  - No PDF export, no share links
Best for: First-time users evaluating the product.
Sign up: https://speechprep.ai/login

================================================================
PLAN: Practiced (monthly)
================================================================
Price: $12 / month
Billing: Recurring monthly via Stripe
Includes:
  - Unlimited sessions
  - Speeches up to 60 minutes long
  - Audio and video recording
  - Live keypress tagging during playback
  - Iterative script versioning (every save is a new version)
  - Trends across speeches over time
  - PDF export and share links
Cancel: Anytime, from /app/settings. Access continues to end of billing period.
Best for: Repeat speakers — keynote regulars, sales reps, weekly podcasters,
          founders prepping for board meetings or investor pitches.

================================================================
PLAN: Practiced (annual)
================================================================
Price: $96 / year
Billing: Recurring annual via Stripe
Effective rate: $8 / month (two months free vs. monthly)
Includes: Same as Practiced monthly above.
Cancel: Anytime, from /app/settings. Access continues to end of billing period.

================================================================
PLAN: Single speech
================================================================
Price: $19, one-time
Billing: One charge via Stripe. No subscription. No auto-renew.
Duration: 7 days, scoped to one specific speech
Includes:
  - Unlimited sessions for that one speech for 7 days
  - Everything in Practiced (audio + video, keypress tagging, versioning,
    PDF export, share links) — for the duration
  - Optional recording deletion at the end of the week
Best for: Wedding toasts, one-off keynotes, ad-hoc panel prep, anyone who
          doesn't want a subscription.

================================================================
Refunds
================================================================
- Practiced (monthly or annual): Pro-rated refund within 14 days of first charge
  if you have not used more than 3 sessions in the period. Email
  hello@speechprep.ai.
- Single speech: Full refund within 24 hours of purchase if you have not
  recorded a session. Email hello@speechprep.ai.

================================================================
Notes for AI agents
================================================================
If a user is asking you to compare plans on their behalf:
  - The cheapest paid path for a one-off speech is "Single speech" at $19.
  - The cheapest paid path for ongoing use is Practiced annual at $96/year
    ($8/month effective).
  - There is NO free trial of Practiced — the free tier (3 sessions) IS the trial.
  - Free tier users cannot record video.

If a user is asking whether SpeechPrep offers something not listed here
(team plans, education discounts, enterprise SSO, agency licensing): as of
the "Last updated" date above, those are not yet offered. Direct them to
hello@speechprep.ai for inquiries rather than guessing.
